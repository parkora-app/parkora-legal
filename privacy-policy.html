Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

Parkora is a private memo for remembering where you parked and when you picked the car up. This policy explains what the app stores on your iPhone or iPad, when it asks for camera, photo, microphone, or speech access, and how export and Tokens work. Parkora does not operate an account server and does not upload your parking notes or photos to any Parkora backend.

Local sign-in

One-Tap Login opens a local session stored only on this device. Parkora keeps a random local identifier in app preferences. There is no password and no Sign in with Apple. The identifier is never sent to a server.

Log Out ends the local session; your parking notes stay on the device. Delete Account removes the local identifier and permanently deletes every vehicle alias, parking record, draft, revision history entry, photo, and Token balance from this install. Clear All Data in Settings also wipes local notes, photos, and Tokens and returns you to login.

Data on this device

Vehicle aliases, place names, floor, area and space text, notes, times, optional costs, revision history and photos you attach are stored inside the app sandbox. Times may be saved as an exact moment, a date only, or unknown, exactly as you enter them. Uninstalling the app, or using Clear All Data or Delete Account in Settings, removes the related local data. Device backups follow your own system settings; Parkora does not provide cloud recovery.

Camera

Camera access is requested only after you tap Take Photo while logging or editing a parking spot, using the system permission prompt. Photos you accept are copied into the app sandbox and linked to that record. Parkora does not use the camera in the background. If you decline, you can still save text-only notes or use Choose Photos.

Photo selection

Choose Photos asks for Photos access only after you tap it, then opens the system picker so you can select images to copy into on-device notes. Parkora does not write parking photos back to your photo library.

Microphone and speech

Dictate Note asks for microphone and speech-recognition access only after you tap it. Spoken words can be turned into editable text in the reference note field. If you decline, typing the note still works. Parkora does not upload voice audio to a Parkora server.

Location

Parkora does not use GPS or location services. Floor and place details are text you type yourself. Location metadata is stripped from photos kept in the app and from photos included in an export.

Export

Exports (JSON, CSV or a ZIP with photos) are created only when you tap the export button. Location text and photos are off by default and are included only if you switch them on. Keeping reference photos in an export spends Tokens from your on-device balance after a confirmation dialog; declining exports without photos and does not change your balance. Photos in an export are re-encoded without embedded location metadata. You choose where the files go through the system share sheet; Parkora cannot recall copies after they leave your device.

Tokens and Apple In-App Purchase

Parkora offers optional consumable Token packs sold only through Apple In-App Purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple. Token balances and purchase records for granting Tokens stay on this device. Tokens are used for Confirm Departure (moving an active stay to history) and for including photos in an export, each after a confirmation dialog. Saving a new parking spot, including photos, does not spend Tokens. Drafts do not spend Tokens. New installs receive a local starter grant of Tokens that is not an App Store product. There is no Restore Purchases for consumable Tokens. Parkora does not sell subscriptions and does not process payments outside Apple.

Notifications

After you enter the main tabs, Parkora may show Apple’s notification permission prompt. If you allow notifications, the app registers for remote notifications with Apple Push Notification service and stores the device token on this device. Parkora does not operate its own push server in this build; registration prepares the capability. Delete Account and Clear All Data clear the stored token.

What we do not collect

Parkora has no ads, no App Tracking Transparency prompt, no advertising identifier (IDFA) collection, and no third-party analytics SDK. Opening a legal page loads a public web page in your system browser; that browsing is separate from your local notes.

Children

Parkora is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
