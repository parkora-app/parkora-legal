Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

Parkora is a private memo for remembering where you parked and when you picked the car up. This policy explains what the app stores on your iPhone or iPad, when it asks for camera, photo, microphone or speech access, and how export works. Parkora does not operate an account server and does not upload your parking notes or photos to any Parkora backend.

Local sign-in

One-tap login opens a local session stored only on this device. Parkora keeps a random local identifier in app preferences. There is no password and no Sign in with Apple. The identifier is never sent to a server.

Log Out ends the local session; your parking notes stay on the device. Delete Account removes the local identifier and permanently deletes every vehicle alias, parking record, draft, revision history entry and photo from this install.

Data on this device

Vehicle aliases, place names, floor, area and space text, notes, times, optional costs, revision history and photos you attach are stored inside the app sandbox. Times may be saved as an exact moment, a date only, or unknown, exactly as you enter them. Uninstalling the app, or using Delete All Records or Delete Account in Settings, removes the related local data. Device backups follow your own system settings; Parkora does not provide cloud recovery.

Camera

Camera access is requested only after you tap Take Photo while logging or editing a parking spot, using the system permission prompt. Photos you accept are copied into the app sandbox and linked to that record. Parkora does not use the camera in the background. Still-photo capture does not enable video or microphone audio. If you decline, you can still save text-only notes, dictate a note, or choose photos with the system picker.

Photo selection

Choose Photos is available while logging or editing a parking spot. After you tap Choose Photos, Parkora shows Apple’s Photos permission prompt if access has not been decided yet. When you allow access, the system photo picker opens; Parkora copies only the images you select into the app sandbox. Parkora does not write parking photos back to your photo library. If you decline, you can still Take Photo or save text-only notes.

Dictate Note (microphone and speech)

Microphone and speech-recognition access are requested only after you tap Dictate Note while writing an optional parking reference note. Spoken words are turned into editable text in that note field on this device. Dictation stops when you tap Stop Dictation or leave the form. You can always type the note instead if you decline these permissions.

Location

Parkora does not use GPS or location services. Floor and place details are text you type yourself.

Export

Exports (JSON, CSV or a ZIP with photos) are created only when you tap the export button. Location text and photos are off by default and are included only if you switch them on. Keeping reference photos in an export spends Tokens from your on-device balance after a confirmation dialog; declining exports without photos and does not change your balance. Photos in an export are re-encoded without embedded location metadata. You choose where the files go through the system share sheet; Parkora cannot recall copies after they leave your device.

Tokens and Apple In-App Purchase

Parkora offers optional consumable Token packs sold only through Apple In-App Purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple. Token balances and purchase records for granting Tokens stay on this device. Tokens are used for optional features such as including photos in an export. New installs receive a local starter grant of Tokens that is not an App Store product. Parkora does not sell subscriptions and does not process payments outside Apple.

Notifications

After you enter the main tabs, Parkora may show Apple’s notification permission prompt. If you allow notifications, the app registers for remote notifications with Apple Push Notification service and stores the device token on this device. Parkora does not operate its own push server in this build; registration prepares the capability. Delete Account clears the stored token.

What we do not collect

Parkora has no ads, no App Tracking Transparency prompt, no advertising identifier (IDFA) collection, and no third-party analytics SDK. Opening a legal page loads a public web page in your browser; that browsing is separate from your local notes.

Children

Parkora is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use Support in the Parkora app Settings.
