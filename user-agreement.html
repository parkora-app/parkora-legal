What Parkora is

Parkora is a free-to-download personal memo for recording where a vehicle is parked and when it was collected. It is a note-taking tool, not a navigation or parking-enforcement service. Optional Token packs are sold only through Apple In-App Purchase for Confirm Departure and for photo exports. Saving a parking spot is free.

Purchases

Token packs are consumable Apple In-App Purchases. Apple handles billing. Tokens stay on this device and are not transferable. There is no restore for consumable Tokens. Refunds follow Apple’s App Store policies. A local starter Token grant on a new install is not an App Store product.

Your notes

Everything you enter stays on your device. You are responsible for the accuracy of what you record and for keeping your own backups. Parkora never invents a time you did not enter; records with unknown times are shown as Not recorded.

Acceptable use

Do not use Parkora to store unlawful content or to record information about other people without their consent. Keep exported files you share in line with the privacy of the people and places they describe.

Account

Sign-in is local to your device via One-Tap Login. You may Log Out at any time. Delete Account or Clear All Data in Settings permanently deletes local notes, photos, and Tokens on this install.
